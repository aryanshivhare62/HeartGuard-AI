## Caveats and Limitations

1. Feature semantics may differ across sites despite sharing column names. Clinical validation of coding consistency is pending.
2. External site datasets (Hungary, Switzerland, VA) are used for zero-shot evaluation only. They must not influence model training, hyperparameter tuning, or threshold selection.
3. Switzerland has a high proportion of zero values in `chol`. The cause of this is not documented in the UCI dataset description and should not be attributed to specific clinical procedures without evidence.
4. Disease prevalence and demographic distributions vary substantially across sites, reflecting genuine population differences.
